Topic

Federated Services vs Event-Driven Projections

Most companies split their software by domain: one service owns people, another owns jobs, another owns security badges, each with its own team and its own database. That keeps teams independent, but a single screen often needs data from several of them at once, and no one service has the whole answer. There are two common patterns for putting it together.

Federated servicesAsk every owning service at the moment of the request and combine the replies.
Event-driven projectionsEach service announces its changes as events; keep a combined copy up to date and answer from it.

These lessons run both against the same data and show what each one costs in speed, freshness and failure.
