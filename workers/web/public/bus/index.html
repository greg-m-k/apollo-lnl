When the bus stops

The event-driven side decouples writes from reads. That holds even when the parts in between fail. Stop the broker or the consumer, keep writing, and watch the source of truth and the projection drift apart, then meet again.

1. A person of your own

Seed people are shared and read-only, so make one to change.

2. Break something

Only for you. Each switch turns itself off after 3 minutes.

The relay cannot publish: events wait in the outbox table, next to the data they describe.
Events are published but nobody applies them: they wait on the queue and are redelivered.

3. Keep writing

Writes go to the HR and Employment services, which do not care whether the bus is up.
